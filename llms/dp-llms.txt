# DP Technology (Bohrium)

> DP Technology (Deep Potential Technology, 深势科技) is a pioneer of "AI for Science," founded in 2018. Its Bohrium platform is a research cloud for microscale scientific computing and industrial design — compute jobs, datasets, container images, knowledge bases, paper/patent/scholar search, PDF parsing, and a scientific-tools library — all reachable through the Bohrium OpenAPI (Bearer AccessKey auth).

## APIs

- [Bohrium OpenAPI 2.0.0](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/dp/refs/heads/main/openapi/dp-bohrium-openapi.json): 166 operations across 15 services (job, node, dataset, file, image, project, knowledge-base, lkm, paper-search, pdf-parser, scholar-search, sciencepedia, tools, web-search, mentor). Base URL https://open.bohrium.com, Authorization: Bearer BOHR_ACCESS_KEY.
